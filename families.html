Family Counseling

When was the last time that you felt truly connected with the members of your family? The work we do together will be specific to your family's needs. We will meet in different constellations to be effective. This means that we can get the whole family together to hear everyone's take on what is going on, we can meet in a parenting session, or a parent-child constellation. At the same time, we will be working on the love and connection between the individuals in your family.

In our world there are so many versions of a family: nuclear, single, extended, step, same-sex, generational, foster, adopted, kinship and I am sure so many more. For me, family is a lasting meaningful relationship with another individual. This broad definition gives us the opportunity to work through all the dynamics of life.

Areas of concentration:

  • Repairing family relationships
  • Parenting
  • Becoming a single parent
  • Adoption
  • Foster Care
  • Kinship
  • Parenting a child with ADD/ADHD
  • Parenting a child with Reactive Attachment Disorder
  • Parenting a child with Oppositional Defiant Disorder
  • Parenting a child with Conduct Disorder
