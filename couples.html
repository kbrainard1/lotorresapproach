Couples Counseling

Do you remember the butterflies that swarmed your stomach when you first got together? When was the last time you and your partner looked at each other and really saw one another? When was the last time the two of you laughed at a shared old feel-good memory? Together we will work towards you feeling close again. We will look at new and old hurts. Your experiences matter! They have an impact on how you feel, how you think, and how you act. We will look at how you approach your partner and how your partner responds to you. We will not chase after who is at fault or who is responsible for the arguments. We will look at your patterns of communication and where you get stuck. No more walking on eggshells or fear of explosions. No more silent treatment or having the feeling of rejection in the pit of your stomach.
