Schedule an Appointment

Counseling that is rooted in Attachment theory will create lasting improvements in all your relationships. No more lists of coping skills that do not help when it gets hard. No more forever counseling. We will work hard to achieve results that you can hold on to. In session we will find some old hurts that never healed, we will sequence your experiences to find out what is going on, we will understand your current approaches and responds to conflict, we will learn about you. Together we will create solutions that will be right for you. Together we will work towards you feeling more connected with your loved ones. I am asking you to take the risk to dare to be vulnerable.
